IMPORTANT

Game Rules

🃏 Rule 01 — Follow the Card Each card's instruction must be followed unless another card specifically changes it.
🪙 Rule 02 — Cards Are Game Money Value cards represent the player's game wealth.
🤝 Rule 03 — Trades Require Agreement Normal trades require both players to agree.
🂡 Rule 04 — Ace Gives Choice The Ace allows a legal choice, but does not remove the basic rules.
🃏 Rule 05 — Joker Has Limits Joker powers follow the specific Joker rules and cannot create unlimited money.
⚖️ Rule 06 — No Unlimited Taking A player cannot take another player's money or assets unless a card specifically allows it.
🔄 Rule 07 — Turns Stay in Order Players take turns clockwise unless a card changes the order.
❤️ Rule 08 — Dreams Guide the Game Players build toward their Dreams while managing their resources and decisions.

WHY THIS PROJECT

Internet access should reach more communities.

A community initiative of TYMA through FWORLD Marketplace.

Did you know?

In the Philippines, internet connection can be slow and some areas remain difficult to access.

With the rapid change of society now, it is the right of every Filipino to access an internet connection.

Here in TYMA, our first campaign is:

“WIFI sa bawat kalye.”

We are exploring community-based opportunities that can help bring accessible internet closer to neighborhoods, communities and everyday Filipino families.

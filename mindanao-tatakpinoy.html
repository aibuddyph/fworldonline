THE MINDANAO STORY

Culture lives in what we create.

Mindanao is home to diverse communities, traditions and artistic expressions. From woven textiles to intricate carvings, metalwork and cultural art, every creation represents a piece of a much bigger story.

Through Tatak Pinoy, FWORLD aims to create a space where these products can be discovered, appreciated and shared with a wider audience.

ABOUT TYMA

You Matter.

TYMA creates a space where women can learn, reflect, connect, and grow together.

Why TYMA?

Sometimes women spend so much time taking care of everyone else that they forget to take care of themselves.


TYMA reminds every woman that her story, dreams, voice, growth and future matter.

Our Purpose

To create a supportive community where women can develop self-worth, confidence, clarity, purpose and practical skills for today's world.


We believe growth becomes more meaningful when women grow together.

Behind the Book

Some stories are written because we need to understand what we have lived.

EQUILIBRIUM is a reflection on love, wandering, self-discovery, detachment, healing, and the journey toward becoming whole.

It is about the spaces between holding on and letting go, between loving someone and remembering how to love yourself.

This book was created from experiences, thoughts, questions, and lessons that slowly became words.

INSIDE THE BOOK

What you'll find between the pages.

Love

Understanding love without losing yourself in the process.

Wandering

The uncertainty, questions, and discoveries that happen along the way.

Becoming Whole

Finding yourself again and creating a life rooted in self-worth.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

AmitafLee
